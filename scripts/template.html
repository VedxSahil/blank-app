Your Indian market morning, in one place

Pre-Market Dashboard is a free one-page view of the Indian stock market before the opening bell. It gathers GIFT Nifty, the Nifty 50 and Sensex previous closes, India VIX, the rupee, Brent crude, overnight US markets and foreign and domestic fund flows, then works out the expected open for you.

Rebuilt every weekday at 6:30 AM IST. The time of the latest update is at the top of the page.

NewsEight market headlines with links to the source, a tone label, and who each story may help or hurt.

How it works

  1. Every weekday at 6:30 AM IST a scheduled GitHub workflow starts. It runs on its own, so no computer of yours needs to be on.
  2. It collects data from Yahoo Finance, NSE India and Google News, all free public sources, so no paid service or API key is involved.
  3. It rebuilds this page and publishes it. Anything it cannot find shows as "not found" instead of a guess.

Good to know

  • Figures are previous closes or the latest available price, not a live feed, and they can be delayed or wrong. Check them before you rely on them.
  • The explorer covers a fixed list and uses closing prices. It has no P/E, market cap or other company fundamentals.
  • Nothing you type into the calculator or search is saved or sent anywhere.
  • This is for learning only. It is not investment advice and does not recommend any stock.

Strategy lab

Learning tool, not advice. This screen only describes past prices. A stock that rose last month can fall next month, and any stock can lose money. Nothing here predicts or promises a return, and the stocks listed are not recommendations to buy.

How the screen picks stocks

  1. Start with the 30 Sensex stocks.
  2. Keep a stock only if it rose over the last month and its latest price is above its 10-day average.
  3. Rank by one-month gain divided by daily price swings (volatility), so steady risers rank above wild ones.
