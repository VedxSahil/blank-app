How the screen picks stocks

  1. Start with the 30 Sensex stocks.
  2. Keep a stock only if it rose over the last month and its latest price is above its 10-day average.
  3. Rank by one-month gain divided by daily price swings (volatility), so steady risers rank above wild ones.
